- Understand: interactive mat/floating cross-section, five controls, provisional observed temperature examples, recorded comparisons and CSV.
- Predict: real evidence explorer separate from reproducible simulated training; editable input selection/history, logistic regression, held-out confusion counts, baseline and downloads.
- Detect: discrete measured visits, separately simulated daily channels, true-colour Sentinel-2 and projected nominal pixel grids, two-pigment optical spectrum and downloadable numbers.
- Identify: licensed microscope/field images, source credits, browser nearest-neighbour teaching classifier, held-out originals and uncertainty quiz.
- Decide: six imperfect-evidence rounds, asymmetric costs, sampling, replay and decision log.
- Four challenges per station, local progress, open project questions, methods/omissions drawers and notebook settings.
- Coach/adaptation guides, model documentation, source registry with 12 collections, MIT/CC BY notices and footer Gmail, LinkedIn, CyanoTracker and sibling links.

## Research review still required

1. Growth, daily sensor timing, training outcomes and decision costs are illustrative and not calibrated. Real data do not train a toxin/safety predictor.
2. City QC flags U/J remain undecoded. Temperature examples are provisional, uneven visit summaries—not climatology or validated slider ranges.
3. The spectrum reuses forward equations and the 620 nm PC term. Analytic reference shapes replace numerical tables whose redistribution rights were not verified. It differs numerically from Water Optics Lab and does not model benthic mats.
4. The classifier collection is deliberately incomplete: four cyanobacteria training originals, with a cyanobacterium and green alga held out. It teaches limitations and wrong labels, not reliable identification. No pollen photo is included. Local OTU plates retain source labels pending genus review.
5. Satellite alignment is a demonstration grid, not a real satellite footprint. The real RGB image and historical outline are not toxin maps.
6. Real toxin data contain different sample media/units and censored values; no missing or below-limit result is treated as safe.

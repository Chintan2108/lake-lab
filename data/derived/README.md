# Station-ready summaries of frozen public records

These are **real observations and derived summaries**, not simulations, forecasts, continuous lake sensors, or swimming-safety advice. Generated by `python3 scripts/derive-station-data.py` without network access. Retrieval dates, exact download requests and original metadata remain in each source folder. `metadata.json` records input/output hashes, source identifiers, counts and derivation date.

## Sources and licence

- City of Austin Watershed Protection, [Environmental Monitoring and Compliance Data, 5tye-7ray](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/5tye-7ray). Public Domain in source metadata. Original frozen source: `../austin-water-quality/metadata.json`.
- NOAA/NCEI GHCN-Daily, Camp Mabry station USW00013958. US government public-domain data; acknowledge NOAA/NCEI. Original frozen source: `../noaa-weather/metadata.json`.
- USGS daily mean discharge, site 08158000, parameter 00060/statistic 00003. USGS public-domain data. Original frozen source: `../usgs-flow/metadata.json`.

The transformation code is MIT. Dataset rights remain those of their sources.

## Files and cleaning rules

Every table has both JSON and CSV versions.

- **water-evidence**: all 1,584 matching water measurements at sites 1996 (Redbud West of Parking Lot), 1252 (Powerline), and 1997 (Lagoon at Festival Beach). These are deliberately selected in-lake HAB sites. The watershed's tributaries, sediment and mat measurements are not pooled into lake-water summaries. Original source fields, result strings, qualifiers, QC flags, depths and row reference numbers remain intact.
- **water-visits**: 1,086 site/day/analyte summaries used by station charts. Only exact parameter/unit pairs and `Surface Water` medium are accepted. Known depth must be 0–0.5 m, `qc_type` must be empty (exclude field splits/other QC samples), result must be numeric and `qualifier` empty. If more than one measurement qualifies per site/day/analyte, use its median, with measurement count, depth range and source reference IDs. In this snapshot every accepted group has one measurement. Exclusions: 430 qualified/censored, 20 missing/deeper depth, 48 nonempty QC type. Exclusions are mutually exclusive, applied in the order in the script.
- **water-monthly**: 53 pooled calendar-month/analyte summaries across years. Median/min/max of site/day values, with counts of site-days, measurements, sites and distinct years. These are unevenly sampled seasonal context, **not monthly climatology**, percentiles or validated slider limits. No interpolation of absent months. Temperature has no January, April or December result; February and March each contain just three site-days. Missing nutrient months and censored values mean nutrient medians are biased toward reported unqualified values. Never treat them as representative population means or use absent values as zero.
- **environment-daily**: 3,653 calendar rows, 2016-01-01 through 2025-12-31. Outer calendar join of Camp Mabry air maximum/minimum temperature (°C), rainfall (mm), and **downstream-of-Longhorn-Dam** USGS flow (ft³/s). One missing flow date, 2017-01-26, remains null. Raw NOAA attribute strings and USGS qualifiers remain attached. No unit conversion, imputation, lagging, rolling-average calculation or toxin labels. Rainfall trace flags are retained; a reported numeric zero with a trace flag is not a claim of no rain. NOAA air temperature is not lake water temperature.
- **toxin-evidence**: 365 original analyte rows at the three selected lake sites, all from 2019. Preserve medium, unit, method, qualifier, sample_ref_no, timestamp and `time_null`. Water (UG/L) and mat (ng/g) are fundamentally different sample types and must never be directly compared as the same concentration.
- **toxin-samples**: 73 groups keyed by site + exact source timestamp + sample_ref_no + medium + unit. These are sample groups, **not 73 independent visits**. Each lists analytes and source row IDs. `below_limit_count` only counts literal `<` qualifiers. `unqualified_count` is not a positive-toxin count. No binary toxic/non-toxic or safe/unsafe target is created. An unspecified qualifier does not prove a verified detection; a below-limit measurement is not zero or proof of safety. Midnight times marked `time_null=Y` are missing collection times.

## QC and interpretation limitations

The source's `qc_flag` codes have not been authoritatively decoded. Accepted summary inputs retain 291 `U`, 33 `J`, and 762 blank flags. The summary must therefore be labelled **provisional; flags retained, meanings not verified**. Do not claim validated QC or quietly interpret `U` as a nondetection. Only the explicit `qualifier` column controls censored-value exclusion here. Full evidence remains downloadable for review.

No selected-site turbidity exists in this frozen set. Do not invent a turbidity channel, claim these discrete visits form continuous day-long records, or compare profile collection times as temporal sensor dynamics. Any day-long sensor exercise must use a separately labelled simulation. Phycocyanin is absent. No sunlight measurement is derived here. No representative whole-year lake-water seasonal cycle or toxin predictor is justified by these samples.

The app should display source sample counts, units, dates and these limitations near any derived chart. Static JSON arrays are intentionally straightforward for small browser modules. Source row IDs provide a path back to the unmodified evidence.
